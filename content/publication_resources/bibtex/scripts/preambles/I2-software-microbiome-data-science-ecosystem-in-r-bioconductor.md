**miaverse** (`microbiome.github.io`) is a microbiome data science ecosystem
built on the Bioconductor `SummarizedExperiment` family, covering
high-throughput analysis, visualization, simulation, time series, interactive
exploration, access to the EBI MGnify and HoloFood resources, and open data
sharing. The ten Bioconductor packages below form its core. The reproducible
online tutorial book *Orchestrating Microbiome Analysis*
(`microbiome.github.io/OMA`) describes how to take advantage of the ecosystem.
In 2021 we provided training on this framework in intensive courses in Oulu,
the Netherlands, Barcelona and at CSC, as well as virtually.
