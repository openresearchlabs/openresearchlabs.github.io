The **Bioconductor framework for microbiome data science**
(`microbiome.github.io`) builds on the `SummarizedExperiment` family of
classes and covers high-throughput analysis, visualization, simulation, time
series, interactive exploration, access to the EBI MGnify and HoloFood
resources, and open data sharing. The ten Bioconductor packages below form its
core. The reproducible online tutorial book *Orchestrating Microbiome
Analysis* (`microbiome.github.io/OMA`) describes how to take advantage of the
framework. In 2021 we provided training on it in intensive courses in Oulu,
the Netherlands, Barcelona and at CSC, as well as virtually.
